## IPO-Ready focus for the next run

IPO-Ready gives retail investors free, evidence-backed financial analysis of companies before they list, when comparable research is harder to find. Focus the deep analysis on a small set of pre-IPO issuers with dated public disclosures. Use listed companies as benchmarks where their business models and financial definitions make the comparison meaningful.

- Start with **two issuers**. Keep **Indian Gas Exchange (IGX)** as one case. Explain how trading volumes and fees drive growth, what caused the swing in operating cash flow, and which comparisons with listed exchanges hold up. [IGX draft prospectus](https://www.sebi.gov.in/filings/public-issues/jul-2026/indian-gas-exchange-limited-drhp_102988.html)
- Review **Oravel Stays (OYO)** as the second candidate. It is already in V2, and its public draft prospectus and October addendum provide a starting point for testing the method on a different business. Confirm the IPO stage and usable disclosures at the analysis cutoff. If coverage is insufficient, select another issuer with verifiable pre-IPO status and enough public filings. Record why. [Oravel updated draft](https://www.sebi.gov.in/filings/public-issues/jun-2026/oravel-stays-limited-udrhp-i_102455.html) and [October addendum](https://www.sebi.gov.in/filings/public-issues/oct-2026/oravel-stays-limited-addendum-to-drhp_105418.html)
- For each issuer, answer four questions. What drives growth? Is profit turning into cash? Which listed peers are genuinely comparable? What can we say about valuation at this stage of the offering? Choose measures that fit the business and trace each material conclusion to the filing, calculation and any evidence that could change the interpretation. Flag missing inputs and distinguish indicative dealer quotes from official offer prices.
- Test the free-research gap directly. Using the same questions and dated information for both issuers, compare what a reader can answer using existing free sources with what they can answer using IPO-Ready. Record what is newly answerable, what becomes better supported, and what remains open. Put the two cases in the reproducible Python notebook and at the center of the demo.
