## Method decisions and limits

- **Growth.** Compare accounting growth with an independent operating KPI. The fixed-prior-rate IGX bridge isolates volume and leaves rate/mix residual. An additive G6 contribution handles Oravel's acquisition discontinuity. Comparing headline revenue growth alone cannot isolate either effect. Try contract-level or organic cohorts when those disclosures are available.
- **Cash.** IGX isolates a material working-capital contribution. Oravel aligns reported CFO, capex, borrowing interest and lease cash paid. Direct CFO/PAT would present 3.73× for IGX and 2.53× for Oravel while leaving the source of those readings unexplained. Keep FY2025 Oravel closure within disclosed precision, with activity and closing residuals of −₹0.001 crore and a separate ₹0.002 crore discrepancy between closing cash and its composition total.
- **Comparison and valuation.** A broad group-multiple grid is useful for candidate discovery. Metric-level gates admit a narrower reference without forcing a composite rank or relying on sector labels alone. Indicative reported EPS and conditional equity value are observable arithmetic. Deducting tax benefits or using EV/EBITDA needs reviewed normalisation of non-recurring earnings, shares and lease-adjusted enterprise value. Await the official band and terms before translating either dealer quote into an offer decision.
- **Acquisition.** The tool preserves the dated raw facts and receipts. If the Oravel annual PDF is missing or corrupt, the financial view uses available provider records and the revised valuation is withheld. The full annual PDF was reduced to original source pages of interest for offline use. The notebook and the browser reference the original physical page numbers.
