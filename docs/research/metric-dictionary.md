# Initial metric dictionary

Status: proposed definitions informed by the [Aether pilot](pilots/aether-ipo-2022.md), not a universal taxonomy mapping.

| Metric | Working definition | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operating revenue | Revenue from operations | Separate from total income and collections |
| Other income | Separately disclosed non-operating income | Never assume missing means zero |
| Total income | Source-reported total income | Reconcile to components without relabeling it revenue |
| PAT | Source profit after tax | Continuing/total operations, owners/group and OCI treatment explicit |
| Operating EBITDA | Formula adopted for that analysis | Preserve issuer-reported EBITDA and its reconciliation separately; other income, exceptional items and lease treatment matter |
| Borrowings | Current plus non-current borrowings | Include/exclude leases explicitly; incomplete components yield a partial total |
| Net debt | Named cash/debt formula | Issuer-defined net debt may differ from financing debt less eligible cash; restrictions and investments need review |
| CFO | Net cash from operating activities | Distinguish before/after tax and quarter/YTD; preserve source cash-flow classification |
| EPS | Source EPS with its earnings/share basis | Basic/diluted, annual/interim/TTM/multi-year weighted and pre/post-offer shares |
| CAGR | Positive endpoints over actual elapsed years | Missing fiscal observations must not shorten the denominator; negative endpoints need a different treatment |
| Cash conversion | CFO/PAT over identical periods | Loss/zero PAT and missing inputs handled explicitly |
| P/E | Price and earnings/share basis at named dates | Source price date, EPS period, share count, dilution and peer basis |
| Financial-strength score | Not selected | Explanation is not calibration; coverage is not predictive confidence |

## Source-selection rules to test

Choose facts by decoded period, basis, dimensions and unit. A familiar tag name or context ID is only a candidate. Version mappings by taxonomy and issuer exceptions; keep raw facts so a corrected mapping does not require refetching.
