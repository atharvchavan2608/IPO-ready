# U03: Public availability boundary for IPO drafts

- Type/publisher: official staff processing guidance, SEC Division of Corporation Finance.
- Source: [Enhanced accommodations for draft registration statements](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/draft-registration-statement-processing-procedures-expanded).
- Source dates: original August 17, 2017 header; revised guidance March 3, 2025. Retrieved/checked: 2026-10-22.
- Review basis: selected official sections and native HTML.
- Location: “Securities Act initial public offerings (IPOs) and initial Exchange Act registrations”.

## Observation

The described nonpublic initial-registration process calls for public filing at least 15 days before the road show, or requested effectiveness when no road show occurs. Initial submission and public release can therefore differ.

## Interpretation and limits

A free public API cannot expose unreleased drafts. Record public-availability evidence separately from the draft's submission date. This is a data-planning implication of selected guidance, not an exhaustive legal eligibility review. Do not generalise this timing to every subsequent offering.
