# X03: MCA statutory XBRL route and taxonomy families

- Type: official/jurisdiction reference.
- Publishers: XBRL India and Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
- Checked on: 2026-10-22.
- Sources: [XBRL India taxonomy overview](https://in.xbrl.org/about-us/xbrl-taxonomies/) and [MCA Ind AS filing manual](https://www.mca.gov.in/Ministry/pdf/Filing_Manual_IndAS_V1.0.pdf).
- Review depth: taxonomy overview and filing-workflow excerpt.

## Observed finding

The overview distinguishes C&I and Ind AS taxonomies for MCA financial-statement filing. The manual describes instance creation, validation, pre-scrutiny and comparison with adopted financial statements.

## Interpretation and use

MCA statutory statements and exchange financial-result filings are separate acquisition routes. Investigate their coverage independently.

## Limits and next investigation

This is not a current legal-eligibility determination. Public retrieval, cost and complete current taxonomy packages have not been tested. Resolve those before treating MCA as a dataset source.
