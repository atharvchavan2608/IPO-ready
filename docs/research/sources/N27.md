# N27 · Public allocation and portfolio-risk methods

Reviewed 25 November 2026.

| Source and location | Observation used |
| --- | --- |
| SEBI Investor, **Factors to Consider Before Investing**, bullets on diversification, asset allocation and investment horizon. No publication date shown. [Official page](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/investment-thingsbeforeinv.html) | Asset mix depends on objectives, horizon and risk tolerance. Diversification uses different exposures. Review when investor needs or exposures change. |
| SEBI Investor, **Asset Allocation Calculator**, equity and fixed-income return inputs and explanatory notes. No publication date shown. [Official calculator](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/calculators/Assets_Allocations.html) | Returns are assumption inputs. The page cautions against treating past equity returns as a forecast or interpreting higher equity weights as necessarily increasing returns. |
| Harry Markowitz, **Portfolio Selection**, March 1952, *Journal of Finance* 7(1), pp. 79–82. [Original paper scan](https://docs.cpuc.ca.gov/PublishedDocs/SupDoc/A1805005/1666/223643143.pdf) | An asset's contribution to portfolio variance depends on cross-asset covariance. The highest-expected-return holding alone does not capture the diversification decision. |
| NSE Indices, **Nifty 1D Rate** factsheet, 30 September 2026, page 1, Statistics and Index Methodology. [Official factsheet](https://www.niftyindices.com/Factsheet/Factsheet_Nifty_1D_Rate.pdf) | The overnight-rate index uses TREPS and returned 5.24% over the previous year. The benchmark statistic is a calibration reference for a scenario assumption, separate from the fund's own one-year figure measured at an earlier date. |

These sources motivate risk-profile design, a shorter-horizon exposure reduction and separate hypothetical return assumptions. The targets, liquidity cap and shocks are project assumptions. The literature does not establish that those percentages are suitable for a specific investor. [Method and experiments](../topics/v21-allocation.md)
