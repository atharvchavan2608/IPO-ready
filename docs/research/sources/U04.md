- Type/publisher: official form instructions, US SEC.

## Observation

The form covers specified foreign-private-issuer registrations and calls for EDGAR filing subject to stated exceptions. It provides a route beyond US-incorporated businesses.

## Interpretation and limits

Include eligible foreign SEC filers in candidate discovery; independently establish IPO status, accounting basis, currency and comparable periods. An F-1 is not by itself proof of an initial offering. No global private-company coverage is established.
