## Observation

The page distinguishes public trading from public disclosure obligations and identifies multiple routes to reporting status.

## Interpretation and limits

Keep issuer reporting status, offering stage and listing status separate. “Pre-IPO” does not tell us whether statements are public or whether shares are available to ordinary investors. Do not assume all private companies have EDGAR financials. The source does not quantify the project's reachable pre-IPO population.
