# X10: HiFi-KPI

- Type: research paper and dataset reference.
- Authors: Rasmus T. Aavang et al.
- First submitted: 2025-02-21; reviewed page version v5, revised 2026-08-20.
- Checked on: 2026-10-22.
- Source: [HiFi-KPI: A Dataset for Hierarchical KPI Extraction from Earnings Filings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15411).
- Review depth: abstract only; code/data not downloaded.

## Finding

Studies hierarchical KPI classification and structured extraction from earnings filings. The abstract highlights date-related extraction errors.

## Interpretation and use

Evaluation should require correct metric, date/period and structure together. Hierarchical labels may help distinguish broad metric families from exact reporting concepts.

## Limits and next investigation

Not an Indian IPO dataset. Read the full paper and inspect its annotation scheme before adopting any benchmark or reported performance comparison.
