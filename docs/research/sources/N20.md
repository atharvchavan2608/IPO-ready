# N20 · NSE MCX split and common portfolio price observations

NSE listing circular `NSE/CML/72042`, dated 29 December 2025, gives the equity face value change from ₹10 to ₹2 effective 2 January 2026 on PDF page 1. The PDF was retained on 24 November 2026 IST. [Listing circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CML72042.pdf) · [Capture manifest and hash](../../../data/captures/portfolio_terms/20261123T183201Z/manifest.json)

`HDFCBANK`, `GOLDBEES` and `MCX` use original NSE bhavcopy `CLOSE_PRICE` in INR and `TURNOVER_LACS` in ₹ lakh. The local CSVs contain selected symbols. The store index records exchange URLs, capture timestamps, and full-file hashes. There are 49 common weekly closes from 21 November 2025 to 20 November 2026. To put MCX prices on the current share basis, Python divides the pre-split closes by five. For example, the raw close of ₹11,052 on 26 December 2025 becomes ₹2,210.40. The following weekly close, ₹2,216 on 2 January 2026, stays on its reported basis.
