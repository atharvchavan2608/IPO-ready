## Authors / publisher / citation

Amiyatosh K. Purnanandam and Bhaskaran Swaminathan. Review of Financial Studies 17(3), 811–848. DOI 10.1093/rfs/hhg055.

## Publication year

2004

## Market / task

Over 2,000 US IPOs, 1980–1997

## Observed finding

Publisher abstract reports offer-price overvaluation of 14%–50%, depending on peer matching, despite positive first-day returns.

## Review basis / method

Compare offer valuation with industry peer multiples. Abstract reviewed.

## Learning / interpretation

Listing gains and relative fundamental valuation answer different questions.

## Potential project use

Separate valuation range from listing-return outcome. Vary peer matching and show the sensitivity.

## Limitations / transfer risks

US historical evidence is not a result about Indian IPOs. Estimates depend on peer criteria. Full methods not reviewed.
