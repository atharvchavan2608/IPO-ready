## Observed finding

Maps fin_total_income into revenue fields. Selects the first two March year-end columns without sorting. Missing numeric PAT compares as False in the loss flag. Dilution measures share-count expansion.

## Review basis / method

Read build_dataset.py. Probed unsorted periods and missing-PAT expression. Calculated both dilution definitions: 100 to 125 shares gives 25% expansion and 20% old-holder dilution.

## Learning / interpretation

Identical labels can hide different financial definitions.

## Potential project use

Sort parsed dates. Keep nullable flags. Name dilution formulas explicitly and distinguish total income from operating revenue.

## Limitations / transfer risks
