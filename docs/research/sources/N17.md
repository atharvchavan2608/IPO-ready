## Observations

Crunchbase gives private-market funding fields their own section, including last funding date, amount and type. IPO status and date, stock symbol and exchange are under a separate public-market section.

## Design decision

Show market status and source separately from financial comparison scope. The prototype's domain selector supports discovery and its side-by-side table gives each admitted company a stated financial perimeter. The screener uses its own annual thresholds and indicative dealer valuation fields. This research uses public help content as a layout and terminology reference.
