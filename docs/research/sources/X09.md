# X09: FinTagging

- Type: research paper.
- Authors: Yan Wang et al.
- First submitted: 2025-05-27; reviewed page version v5, revised 2026-05-17.
- Checked on: 2026-10-22.
- Source: [FinTagging: Benchmarking LLMs for Extracting and Structuring Financial Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20650).
- Review depth: abstract only.

## Finding

Separates financial numeric identification from concept linking across the US GAAP taxonomy and emphasizes structure-aware evaluation.

## Interpretation and use

Evaluate value extraction separately from canonical metric mapping. A correct number assigned to the wrong financial concept is still an error.

## Limits and next investigation

US GAAP benchmark; no demonstrated transfer to Indian exchange or MCA taxonomies. Read the full methods and dataset terms before borrowing an evaluation protocol.
